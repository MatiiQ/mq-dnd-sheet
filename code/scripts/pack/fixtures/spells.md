# Invented spells in reference/'s format (names may match real spells; every text here is made up)

## Spell Descriptions

### Hunter's Mark (PHB p.901)
- **Level/School:** 1 Divination
- **Classes:** Ranger
- **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
- **Effect:** A glowing sigil hovers over one creature; each of your hits on it deals an extra 1d6 Force damage.
- **Higher Levels / Cantrip Upgrade:** The sigil lingers longer with higher slots.

### Melf's Acid Arrow (PHB p.902)
- **Level/School:** 2 Evocation
- **Classes:** Wizard
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a sour leaf)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Effect:** Make a ranged spell attack; a hit deals 4d4 Acid damage that fizzes.
- **Higher Levels:** Damage +1d4 per slot level above 2.

### Glimmer Pulse (PHB p.903)
- **Level/School:** Cantrip Evocation
- **Classes:** Sorcerer, Wizard
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Effect:** A pulse of light washes over a 10-foot cube; each creature in it makes a Con save or takes 1d8 Radiant damage.
- **Cantrip Upgrade:** Damage +1d8 at levels 5, 11 and 17.
