## Class Features (PHB p.902)

- Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

- Bonus Action: a candle flame dances on your palm.

### Level 5: Kindle (PHB p.903)
- The flame grows.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement (PHB p.903)
