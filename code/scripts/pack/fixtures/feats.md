# Invented feats in reference/'s format

## General Feats (PHB p.901)

### Lantern Ward (PHB p.901)
- **Category:** General
- **Prerequisite:** Level 4+; Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma 13+
- **Repeatable:** No
- **ASI:** Int, Wis, or Cha +1
- **Benefits:**
  - **Glow.** You shed a little light.
  - **Ward.** Advantage on saves against darkness.

### Shield Bearer (PHB p.902)
- **Category:** General
- **Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **ASI:** Str +1
- **Benefits:**
  - **Brace.** Plant your feet.
